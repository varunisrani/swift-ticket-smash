# Product Mission

## Pitch

Swift Ticket Smash is a modern facility management platform that helps building operations teams efficiently track, prioritize, and resolve maintenance requests by providing real-time ticket management and intelligent workflow automation.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Facility Management Companies**: Organizations managing multiple commercial or residential properties who need centralized ticket tracking
- **Corporate Building Operations**: In-house teams managing office facilities, warehouses, and corporate campuses
- **Property Management Firms**: Companies handling maintenance requests for rental properties and HOAs

### User Personas

**Mike, Operations Manager** (35-50 years old)
- **Role:** Facility Operations Manager
- **Context:** Manages a team of 15 technicians across 3 office buildings
- **Pain Points:** Losing track of urgent requests, inefficient paper-based systems, difficulty prioritizing work orders
- **Goals:** Reduce response times, improve team productivity, maintain SLA compliance

**Sarah, Maintenance Technician** (25-40 years old)
- **Role:** Field Service Technician
- **Context:** Handles 10-15 maintenance tickets daily across multiple locations
- **Pain Points:** Unclear work priorities, lack of ticket history, poor communication with requesters
- **Goals:** Complete more tickets efficiently, reduce return visits, have clear work instructions

**Jennifer, Office Manager** (30-45 years old)
- **Role:** Administrative Office Manager
- **Context:** Submits facility issues on behalf of 200+ employees
- **Pain Points:** No visibility into request status, can't track recurring issues, emergency requests get lost
- **Goals:** Quick issue resolution, transparent communication, historical tracking for vendors

## The Problem

### Fragmented Ticket Management

Facility teams rely on disconnected systems like email, phone calls, and spreadsheets to manage maintenance requests. This leads to lost tickets, delayed responses, and frustrated occupants who can't track their requests.

**Our Solution:** Centralized ticket system with real-time tracking and automated routing.

### Inefficient Work Prioritization

Without proper categorization and priority scoring, urgent safety issues get buried under routine requests. Teams waste time on low-impact tasks while critical problems escalate.

**Our Solution:** Smart priority algorithms that consider safety, impact, and SLA requirements.

### Poor Communication Loops

Requesters submit tickets into a black hole with no updates. Technicians lack context and often need multiple site visits. This results in 40% longer resolution times.

**Our Solution:** Built-in chat, status updates, and automated notifications keep everyone informed.

## Differentiators

### AI-Powered Triage

Unlike traditional ticketing systems that rely on manual categorization, we use AI to automatically classify tickets, suggest priorities, and route to the right technician based on skills and availability. This reduces initial response time by 60%.

### Mobile-First Design

While competitors focus on desktop dashboards, our mobile-optimized interface allows technicians to update tickets in real-time from the field. This provides instant visibility and reduces administrative overhead by 2 hours per technician daily.

### Predictive Maintenance Insights

Beyond reactive ticket management, we analyze patterns to identify recurring issues and predict equipment failures. This helps facilities transition from break-fix to preventive maintenance, reducing emergency tickets by 35%.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Intelligent Ticket Routing:** Automatically assigns tickets based on category, location, and technician expertise
- **Real-Time Status Tracking:** Live updates on ticket progress with estimated completion times
- **Priority Scoring System:** Multi-factor prioritization considering safety, impact, and service agreements
- **Quick Ticket Submission:** Simple form with photo uploads and voice-to-text descriptions
- **SLA Management:** Track and enforce service level agreements with automated escalations

### Collaboration Features

- **In-Ticket Chat:** Direct communication between requesters and technicians without email chains
- **Team Assignment:** Collaborate on complex tickets with multiple technician assignments
- **Knowledge Base Integration:** Access repair guides and historical solutions within tickets

### Analytics Features

- **Performance Dashboards:** Track KPIs like response time, resolution rate, and technician productivity
- **Trend Analysis:** Identify recurring issues and seasonal patterns for better resource planning
