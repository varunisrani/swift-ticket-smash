# Product Mission (Lite)

Swift Ticket Smash is a modern facility management platform that helps building operations teams efficiently track, prioritize, and resolve maintenance requests by providing real-time ticket management and intelligent workflow automation.

The platform serves facility managers, maintenance technicians, and office administrators who need to streamline maintenance operations across multiple properties. Unlike traditional ticketing systems that rely on manual processes, Swift Ticket Smash uses AI-powered triage to automatically categorize and route tickets, reducing response times by 60% while providing real-time visibility through a mobile-first interface that saves technicians 2 hours of administrative work daily.
